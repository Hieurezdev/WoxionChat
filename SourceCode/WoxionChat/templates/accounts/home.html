About WoxionChat

Documents should be easy to understand

WoxionChat is a simple workspace where you upload your documents and chat with them. Instead of reading page after page, you ask a question and get a clear answer, with a link back to the source.

Everything stays in one calm place: your files, your questions and your past conversations.

  • Save time finding what matters
  • Check every answer against its source
  • Keep all your documents and chats organised
