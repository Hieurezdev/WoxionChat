What are the termination terms in the vendor agreement, and how do they compare to our master services agreement?
Termination terms at a glance

Both agreements allow either party to terminate for convenience, but the notice and cure periods differ meaningfully. 1 2

  • For convenience: 90 days' written notice in the Vendor Agreement, versus 60 days in the MSA. 1
  • For cause: a 30-day cure period begins once written notice of a material breach is received. 2
  • On exit: all outstanding invoices become payable within 15 days of the termination date.
Term Vendor Agreement MSA
Convenience notice 90 days 60 days
Cure period 30 days 45 days
Final payment due 15 days 30 days

In short, the Vendor Agreement gives you more lead time but requires faster final payment.
